How this workflow prioritizes inquiries

Hot inquiries have high volume or urgent buying signals. Warm inquiries show qualified interest or moderate volume. Cold inquiries are lower urgency or lower volume.

Hot 300+ lbs/month or strong urgency/growth signals.
Warm 100+ lbs/month, qualified status, referral, quote, pricing, or partner signals.
Cold Lower volume or lower urgency. Review after higher-priority inquiries.
Contacted Saved in localStorage, so the state remains after refreshing the page.
